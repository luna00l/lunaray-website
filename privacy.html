DATA HANDLING & TRANSPARENCY

PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026 • For Indian & International Clients

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a project inquiry or support request through our website forms, we collect only the information you enter into the form fields:

  • Contact Details: Your Full Name, Email Address, and optional Creator / Brand Handle.
  • Project Specifications: Project Type, Target Platform, Number of Videos, Estimated Target Duration, Budget Range, optional Reference Inspiration Links, and your Project Description / Creative Notes.
  • Footage & Asset Submissions (Optional):
    • If you use the direct file upload option (up to 500 MB), the video, audio, image, project, or archive files you select are uploaded to our backend storage bucket.
    • If you paste a Google Drive file or folder link (recommended for projects exceeding 500 MB), only the URL string you provide is saved with your inquiry record; our website does not automatically download or copy your Google Drive files into our database.
  • Submission Metadata: Confirmation that you accepted the project Terms (`terms_accepted`), the form section from which you initiated the inquiry (`source`), and the server-generated submission timestamp (`created_at`).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information and files you submit solely to:

  • Review your project requirements and assess timeline availability.
  • Communicate with you via email regarding quotations, project scope, revisions, payments, and delivery.
  • Access and edit the raw footage and creative assets you supply for your video project.
  • Respond to support, cancellation, refund, or privacy requests.

We do not use your project inquiries or uploaded footage for unrelated third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. Service Providers & Infrastructure Used

Based on our website's technical implementation, the following service providers process or host data when you use this website:

  • Supabase (Database, Edge Functions & Cloud Storage): Project inquiry records are stored in our Supabase PostgreSQL database (`public.project_inquiries`). Direct file uploads (up to 500 MB) are processed via our Supabase Edge Function (`upload-inquiry-asset`) and stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket (`project-assets`).
  • Google Drive (When Used by Client): For footage shared via Google Drive links or shared directly with lunaray.studi0000@gmail.com, file hosting and access permissions are governed by Google Drive and your Google account settings.
  • Email Provider (Gmail): Direct email communications sent to or from lunaray.studi0000@gmail.com are processed through Google's email infrastructure.
  • Frontend CDNs (Google Fonts & Tailwind CSS CDN): Your browser loads fonts and stylesheet scripts from Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com` / `fonts.gstatic.com`) and Tailwind CDN (`cdn.tailwindcss.com`), which receive standard HTTP request headers (such as your IP address and browser user-agent) when loading the page.

4. Data Protection, Retention & Deletion

  • Access Controls: Our Supabase database (`public.project_inquiries`) has Row Level Security (RLS) enabled so anonymous website visitors can submit new inquiries (`INSERT`) but cannot list or read other clients' submissions (`SELECT`). Direct footage uploads are stored in a non-public Supabase Storage bucket (`project-assets`, `public = false`) where anonymous file listing is disabled, and downloads require a cryptographically signed URL generated by our backend Edge Function.
  • Data Retention & Deletion: Automatic scheduled deletion is not currently configured on our Supabase database or storage bucket. Submitted inquiry records and directly uploaded files remain stored in our Supabase project until they are manually reviewed and deleted by the studio owner or upon a verified client deletion request.   • Google Drive Links: If you share footage via Google Drive, you remain in control of the link and can revoke sharing permissions in your Google Drive account at any time.

5. Requesting Access, Correction, or Deletion

You may contact us at any time to request:

  • Access to a copy of the inquiry details we hold associated with your email address.
  • Correction of inaccurate contact or project information.
  • Deletion of your project inquiry record and/or any raw footage files you uploaded to our Supabase Storage bucket, subject to any records we are required to retain for active projects, invoicing, tax, or dispute resolution under applicable law.
