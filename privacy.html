Privacy Overview • Direct Email Communication

LunaRay Studio operates as a lightweight portfolio and video editing service website. We do not use website registration accounts, user tracking databases, or on-site file upload forms. All project enquiries and support communications take place directly via email.

DATA HANDLING & TRANSPARENCY

PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026 • For Indian & International Clients

1. Information We Receive

When you click a contact, booking, custom edit, or support button on our website, your default email application opens a pre-addressed email to lunaray.studi0000@gmail.com. We only receive the information you choose to send us by email, such as:

  • Contact Details: Your Name, Email Address, and optional Creator / Brand Handle.
  • Project Specifications: Package or Custom Edit preferences, target platform, estimated duration, reference links, and creative notes you include in your message.
  • Footage & Asset Links (Optional): Cloud storage links (such as Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer) that you share with us by email so we can access your raw footage and project assets.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information and footage links you email to us solely to:

  • Review your project requirements and assess timeline availability.
  • Communicate with you via email regarding quotations, project scope, revisions, payments, and delivery.
  • Access and edit the raw footage and creative assets you supply for your video project.
  • Respond to support, cancellation, refund, or privacy requests.

We do not use your project enquiries or footage for unrelated third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. Service Providers & Infrastructure Used

Based on our website's technical implementation, only the following standard services are involved when you browse the website or email us:

  • Email Provider (Gmail): Direct email communications sent to or from lunaray.studi0000@gmail.com are processed through Google's email infrastructure.
  • Google Drive (When Used by Client): For footage shared via Google Drive links or shared directly with lunaray.studi0000@gmail.com, file hosting and access permissions are governed by Google Drive and your Google account settings.
  • Frontend Hosting & CDNs (Vercel, Google Fonts & Tailwind CSS CDN): Our static website is hosted on Vercel, and your browser loads fonts and stylesheet scripts from Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com` / `fonts.gstatic.com`) and Tailwind CDN (`cdn.tailwindcss.com`), which receive standard HTTP request headers (such as your IP address and browser user-agent) when loading the page.

4. Data Protection, Retention & Deletion

  • Confidentiality: Raw footage, unreleased content, and project briefs shared with us via email are kept strictly confidential and used solely to complete your video editing project.
  • Email & Project File Retention: Email correspondence and downloaded working project files are retained only as needed to complete your project, provide revision support, or meet applicable invoicing and legal record-keeping obligations, and are deleted upon verified client request.
  • Google Drive Links: If you share footage via Google Drive or another cloud provider, you remain in full control of the link and can revoke sharing permissions in your cloud account at any time.

5. Requesting Access, Correction, or Deletion

You may contact us at any time to request:

  • Access to details of the project correspondence we hold associated with your email address.
  • Correction of inaccurate contact or project information.
  • Deletion of your email enquiry history and/or any raw footage files held locally for your project, subject to any records we are required to retain for active projects, invoicing, tax, or dispute resolution under applicable law.

To submit a privacy question or a data access, correction, or deletion request, please email us directly or reach out via our Contact & Support page:
