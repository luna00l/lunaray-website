4. Your Content, Your Responsibility (Don't Make It Weird 💀)

Send us your clips, music, and other assets before we start cooking 🎬🔥 To keep things running smoothly, here is what we need from you:

  • The Vision: Send a clear creative brief, target aspect ratio (16:9, 9:16, etc.), and reference videos before we jump in.
  • Working Files: Send clean, uncorrupted files. Direct upload on our site handles up to 500 MB; for bigger footage folders, drop a Google Drive link with download access turned ON 📂.
  • Copyrights & Permissions: You must own or hold the legal license for every clip, track, voiceover, logo, and graphic you supply.

If you tell us to use copyrighted music or unlicensed assets and your video gets claimed, muted, or struck down, that is 100% on you. Let's keep things fair, bestie 🤝.

5. Revisions: Polishing the Cut ✨

We want you to love your cut! The number of revision rounds included is agreed upon upfront in your quote.

Standard revisions: Pacing tweaks, clip swaps, fixing subtitle typos, audio adjustments, and color grade tweaks that fit your original brief.

Scope changes: Need more changes than we agreed on? Dropping brand-new raw footage after the cut is assembled, asking for a totally different aspect ratio halfway through, or doing a complete 180° creative pivot may cost extra. Let's discuss it first 🤝.

6. Deadlines & Turnaround Times ⏱️

We hustle to hit every target date we set. However, timelines depend on teamwork:

  • Late raw footage, broken Drive links, or missing permissions will push the delivery date back.
  • If you ghost us on draft reviews 👻, the timeline pauses until you respond.
  • Extra revision passes or new scope requests will need extra time to render.
  • If an unexpected technical disaster, power outage, or illness hits our side, we will notify you immediately with an updated timeline. Communication is key!

7. Who Owns What (IP & Portfolio Rights) 🎨

  • Your Raw Files: You keep 100% ownership of your raw footage, audio, brand assets, and content. Always.
  • Final Master Delivery: Once agreed project payments are settled, full ownership of the finished exported video transfers to you to post, publish, and monetize. Unless specifically agreed in writing upfront, raw NLE project files (e.g., Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve) are not included in standard delivery.
  • Our Secret Sauce: Our pre-existing editing presets, motion graphics rigs, LUTs, and sound design libraries remain our creative property.
  • Portfolio Flex: We will never show off your completed project on our portfolio, website, or social channels without your explicit permission. Your privacy comes first.

8. Privacy & NDA Energy: We Keep It Secret 🤫

Your unreleased b-roll, private concepts, and communications are safe with us. We never leak, share, or broadcast your footage to anyone. Everything stays locked down strictly for editing your project. Check our Privacy Policy to see how we protect your uploaded files and data.
