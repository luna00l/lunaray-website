TERMS & CONDITIONS

1. Video Editing Services Provided

LunaRay Studio ([OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED: Confirm legal/freelance trade name]) provides freelance post-production and creative editing services for Indian and international clients, including:

  • Short-form vertical video editing (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts).

  • Anime, gaming, and motion-synced edits (AMVs and stylized visual montages).
  • Commercial, brand promotional, and product showcase edits.

  • Custom video thumbnail design.

2. Project Submissions & Quotations

Submitting a project inquiry or uploading footage through this website does not automatically create a binding service contract or guarantee immediate timeline availability. Every submission is reviewed individually based on raw footage volume, creative complexity, requested deliverables, and current studio schedule.

Following review, a tailored project quotation, scope summary, and estimated delivery timeline are sent to the client via email. Editing work begins only after both parties confirm the written scope and payment arrangement in writing via email.

3. Pricing & Payment Arrangements

Any pricing tiers displayed on the website are general starting estimates for reference only. Exact project pricing, currency, payment method, any required advance payment (deposit), milestone payments, and final balance terms are agreed upon personally through email (lunaray.studi0000@gmail.com) before work begins.

4. Client Responsibilities & Material Permissions

To enable smooth production, the client is responsible for:

  • Providing a clear creative brief, target aspect ratio/platform, and reference examples prior to editing.
  • Supplying accessible, non-corrupted footage and assets (via direct upload up to 500 MB on the website or via a shared Google Drive file/folder link with download access enabled for proyectos exceeding 500 MB).
  • Ensuring that the client holds all necessary copyrights, licenses, releases, and permissions for all video clips, audio tracks, voiceovers, logos, fonts, and third-party materials supplied for the project.

The client remains solely responsible for any third-party copyright claims, platform strikes, or legal disputes arising from materials supplied by the client or used at the client's specific instruction.

5. Revisions & Scope Changes

The number of included revision rounds and the terms for any additional editing passes are agreed upon with the client before work begins. Standard revisions cover adjustments to pacing, clip selection, subtitle text, audio mixing, and color tweaks within the originally agreed brief.

Requests that materially change the original scope—such as supplying new raw footage after a cut is assembled, changing the target format/aspect ratio not originally agreed upon, or requesting a complete creative restructuring after brief approval—may require a revised quotation and an adjusted delivery schedule.

6. Estimated Delivery Times & Delays

Estimated delivery timelines depend on the specific project scope and are agreed upon personally with the client before editing starts. While every effort is made to meet agreed target dates, delivery estimates are not absolute guarantees if delays arise from:

  • Delayed delivery of raw footage, assets, or Google Drive sharing permissions by the client.
  • Incomplete instructions or delayed client feedback on review drafts.
  • Additional revision requests or scope changes requested after work has started.
  • Unforeseen technical emergencies, power/internet outages, or illness (in which case the client will be notified promptly).

7. Copyright, Ownership & Portfolio Rights

  • Client Materials: The client retains full ownership of all original raw footage, audio, brand assets, and materials supplied to the studio.
  • Final Deliverables: Ownership and permitted use of the final rendered video deliverable(s) are agreed upon before work begins and transfer to the client upon full completion of the agreed project payment terms. Unless explicitly agreed otherwise in writing, raw NLE project files (e.g., Premiere Pro, After Effects, or DaVinci Resolve project files) are not included in standard video delivery.
  • Pre-Existing Tools & Third-Party Assets: All pre-existing editing templates, presets, LUTs, motion graphics rigs, sound effects libraries, software tools, and third-party stock assets remain the property of the editor or their respective third-party licensors and remain subject to their applicable licenses.
  • Portfolio Display Permission: Completed client projects will not be displayed publicly in the studio portfolio, website, or social media channels without the client's explicit permission.

8. Confidentiality & Handling of Client Footage

Unreleased client footage, private project briefs, and confidential communications are used strictly for the purpose of reviewing and completing the client's editing project. Details on how uploaded files and inquiry data are stored and handled through our website backend are described in our Privacy Policy.

9. Cancellations, Refunds & Right to Decline a Project

Project cancellations and refund requests are handled in accordance with our separate Refund & Cancellation Policy and any project-specific terms agreed via email.

The studio reserves the right to decline an inquiry or cancel a project if:

  • The supplied materials contain unlawful, defamatory, non-consensual, hateful, or infringing content.
  • The client does not hold necessary permissions for the materials provided.
  • Agreed advance payments or milestone terms are not met, or communication remains unresponsive for an extended period.
  • Abusive, harassing, or unethical conduct occurs during collaboration.
