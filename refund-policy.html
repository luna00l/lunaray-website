Submitting an inquiry on our website does not obligate you to make any payment. If you have already confirmed a project via email and wish to cancel before editing, footage ingestion, or timeline preparation has started, please notify us immediately by email at lunaray.studi0000@gmail.com.

Where an advance payment has been made and no editing or preparation work has commenced, refund eligibility will be handled in accordance with the project-specific terms agreed via email prior to payment. 2. Cancellation After Editing Has Started

Video editing involves dedicated timeline labor, footage organization, sound design, and creative assembly. If a client requests to cancel a project after editing work has already begun:

  • We will pause active editing upon receiving your written cancellation request via email.
  • The project status and the portion of editing work already completed up to that point will be reviewed against the agreed project scope and quotation.
  • Any partial refund or balance adjustment will be determined fairly based on the stage of work completed and the payment terms agreed upon in writing before the project began. [OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED: Confirm how you calculate partial refunds or completed-stage charges when a project is cancelled mid-edit.]

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, unpaid drafts or cancelled work-in-progress cuts may not be published or used commercially by the client.

3. Disagreements About Deliverables

Creative editing relies on clear communication and iterative feedback. If a draft deliverable does not match the agreed project brief:

  • Please provide specific, timestamped feedback so the issue can be addressed through the agreed revision rounds.
  • Revisions within the original project scope are always prioritized to ensure the final edit aligns with your brief.
  • If a disagreement remains unresolved after reasonable revision efforts within the agreed scope, both parties will review the work completed and discuss a fair resolution in good faith.

4. Studio Delays or Inability to Complete a Project

If unforeseen studio circumstances prevent us from starting or completing an agreed project within a reasonable timeframe (excluding delays caused by late client footage, missing permissions, or delayed client feedback), we will notify you promptly via email to agree on either an updated delivery schedule or an appropriate refund of payments made for uncompleted work.

5. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund Review

To request a cancellation or refund review, please contact us via email at lunaray.studi0000@gmail.com or through our Contact & Support page with:

  • Your full name and the email address used for the project.
  • The project title or quotation email thread reference.
  • A brief explanation of the reason for the cancellation or refund request.
